## Project Overview

**Takhzeen – More Space for Your Next Move**

Project Overview

Takhzeen is a digital storage marketplace designed to connect businesses in need of flexible storage solutions with warehouse owners who have unused storage capacity.

The Problem

Small businesses, including workshops, small factories, traders, and importers, often require additional storage for goods, raw materials, and equipment. However, traditional warehouse rentals typically involve large spaces and long-term commitments that do not match their short-term or varying storage needs.

As a result, some businesses resort to storing their goods in homes or shops. Meanwhile, warehouse owners struggle to find trustworthy tenants for their unused spaces.

Both parties also face trust-related challenges, including concerns about goods safety, payment reliability, unclear rental terms, and the lack of proper documentation during goods handover.

The Proposed Solution

Takhzeen addresses these challenges through a digital marketplace that makes storage more accessible, flexible, and transparent.

The platform enables businesses to discover and compare available storage units, submit booking requests, sign digital agreements, and make online payments.

It also provides warehouse owners with tools to list and manage their storage units and bookings. To strengthen trust between both parties, the platform supports party verification, goods documentation during check-in and check-out, ratings, and a dispute resolution process.
